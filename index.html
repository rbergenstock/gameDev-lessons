Every question below comes from one of the eight sections of the Phase 1 test. The test pulls a selection of them, so work through all of these and you will have seen everything that can show up.

Answer each one on your own first. The answer key is at the very bottom of this page — check it only after you have tried the whole section. Looking first feels faster and teaches you nothing.
