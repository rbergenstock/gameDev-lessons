Your game is on GitHub. Now three people need it.

You, on a different computer

You're in the lab instead of at home, or the computer wiped your files at logout. You need your project back, with its history.

Your teammate

They need to open your code, change it, and send their changes back to the same repo.

Your teacher

Needs to see it to grade it. Right now your repo is private, so nobody can see it but you.

Those three needs have three different answers: clone, collaborator, and visibility. That's today.
Ask: "Right now, who can open your repo?" (Only them.) That sets up why permissions matter.

Three ways to get code off GitHub

Click each one. Only one of them lets you send changes back.

Rule of thumb: if you plan to change the code, clone it. Download ZIP is a dead end.
Say: "A ZIP is a photocopy. A clone is a branch office that still reports to headquarters."

How to clone a repo

What a clone brings

Every file, every commit, and the link back to GitHub.

That link is why the Push origin button works from the new computer without setting anything up.

You can only clone a repo you're allowed to see.

Model it: clone one of your own repos on the projector. Point out the Local Path, since students often lose track of where the folder landed.

Two computers, one repo

Clone your repo onto both computers, then make changes and watch what moves. Nothing here is real, so experiment.

Run it: clone both, commit on the lab computer, push, then ask "Does the home laptop have it?" (No, until it pulls.) If both sides have unpushed work, Pull is blocked here on purpose. That case is Lesson 4.

Public or private?

Every repo is one or the other. You choose, and you can change it later.

Before you make anything public: no full names, no school ID numbers, no passwords or keys in the code, and nothing you don't have the rights to share. Changing it back to private does not un-share what someone already copied.
Where to change it: repo Settings, then General, then scroll to the Danger Zone and Change repository visibility. Decide as a class before students touch it.

Adding a collaborator

Two sides to it

You send an invitation. Nothing happens until the other person accepts it.

Invitations expire after 7 days.

You can remove a collaborator any time in the same settings page.

Watch for: students inviting a similar username. Have them read the username back to their partner before clicking add.

You haven't accepted the invitation yet. Accept it, then reopen the Clone window.

I already have a folder with that name

Pick a different Local Path, or rename the old folder. Two repos can't share one folder.
